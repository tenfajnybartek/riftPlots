# Grafiki wiki

Baner i logo pochodzą z wcześniejszej oprawy README. Ich prompty są zapisane w [docs/assets/README.md](assets/README.md). Kopie w `docs/wiki/assets/` pozwalają zachować kompletność eksportu do osobnego repozytorium Wiki; Git przechowuje identyczną zawartość jako te same obiekty.

Nowa ilustracja `docs/wiki/assets/city-guide.png` została wygenerowana wbudowanym narzędziem `image_gen` z umiejętności `imagegen` i skopiowana do projektu. Grafiki są koncepcyjne, a nie zrzutami ekranu.

## Prompt nowej ilustracji

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide landscape illustration for the GitHub Wiki of riftPlots, a Minecraft city property plugin.
Primary request: a beautiful architectural overview of a compact Minecraft-inspired city showing different kinds of property in one cohesive isometric voxel diorama.
Subject: a townhouse and an apartment building with two subtly cutaway upper rooms, a shop with a striped awning facing a small plaza, and a modest brick workshop with a warehouse, all on one floating city block. Luminous cyan lines outline distinct rectangular parcels and distinct apartment volumes, suggesting three-dimensional ownership boundaries.
Style: detailed clean voxel 3D editorial illustration, consistent with a premium developer project banner. Crisp buildings, warm lit windows, leafy block trees, clear spatial composition.
Background: deep midnight navy with a restrained architectural grid. Bright cyan #00c6ff and electric blue #0072ff parcel borders. Keep lighting balanced and buildings readable.
Composition: wide landscape 2:1 with comfortable margins. Focus on property layout and city life; avoid crowded details.
Constraints: no text, no labels, no UI, no badges, no watermark, no Minecraft logo. This is a concept illustration, not a game screenshot.
```
